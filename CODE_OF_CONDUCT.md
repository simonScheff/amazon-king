# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for
different viewpoints, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best
for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized
attention, insults, threats, publishing private information, or other conduct
that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct. Serious or
repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's GitHub Security
Advisories. Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and respond in a
fair and timely manner.
