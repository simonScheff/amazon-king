amazon-king software privacy notice

amazon-king is open-source, self-hosted software. The maintainers of the source-code repository do not operate downstream deployments and do not receive their Amazon Advertising data. Each deployment operator is an independent data controller and must publish its own contact details, retention periods, service providers, and legally required disclosures.

Data processed by a deployment

An operator-authorized deployment may process the owner's email address, Amazon Ads account and campaign structure, impressions, clicks, spend, attributed orders and sales, user-entered KDP book economics, application sessions, recommendations, changes, and security audit events.

Purpose and sharing

The software uses this data to synchronize advertising performance, calculate deterministic recommendations, and apply changes explicitly approved by the owner. Data is exchanged with Amazon to provide those functions and may be processed by infrastructure, backup, monitoring, or email providers selected by the deployment operator. It must not be sold or published.

Storage and control

Amazon refresh tokens are encrypted at rest. The operator controls the database, encryption keys, report storage, backups, access controls, retention, export, and deletion. Disconnecting Amazon removes the stored refresh token but does not automatically delete previously imported data. Contact the operator of the specific deployment for access or deletion requests.

This project is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon. Last updated: August 12, 2026.
